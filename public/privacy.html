1. Information we collect

Information you give us at checkout. When you place an order, you enter your name, email address, phone number, shipping address and billing details on a checkout page hosted by our payment processor, Stripe. We receive your order details, contact information and shipping address so we can fulfil your order.

Payment information. Card and other payment details are collected and processed by Stripe. We do not receive or store your full card number.

Information collected automatically. Like most websites, our servers record basic technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type, the pages you request and the date and time of the request. We use it to operate, secure and troubleshoot the site, including limiting repeated checkout attempts.

Information you send us. If you contact us, we keep the contents of your message and your contact details so we can respond.

We do not use your information for targeted advertising, and we do not sell your personal information.

4. Browser storage and cookies

Your cart is saved in your own browser’s local storage so it is still there when you come back. It stays on your device and is not sent to us until you start checkout.
